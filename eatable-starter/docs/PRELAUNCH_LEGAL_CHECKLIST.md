# Eatable — Pre-launch legal and operations checklist

**Not a legal opinion or a finished privacy/terms policy.** Complete each item with the site operator and, where appropriate, an Italy/EU privacy professional before inviting the public.

- [ ] Identify the operator/data controller correctly (natural person or legal entity), contact details and any required VAT/company disclosures.
- [ ] Establish a current inventory of processed data: sign-in, profile, submissions/photos, favorites, pantry, reviews, ratings, problem reports, page visits and system logs.
- [ ] Confirm a lawful basis for each processing purpose, appropriate disclosures, and any consent flows.
- [ ] Document the actual retention/deletion periods for Supabase records, Resend emails, Gmail support reports, provider logs and backups.
- [ ] Check DPA/contract terms, processing locations and international transfers for Supabase, Vercel, Resend, Cloudflare and Gmail.
- [ ] Audit the real deployed site for cookies/localStorage and analytics/advertising scripts; implement consent if applicable. Do not add a cosmetic banner that does nothing.
- [ ] Verify data-subject rights workflow (access, correction, export, deletion, objection/restriction where relevant) and account deletion handling.
- [ ] Confirm licensing/permissions for community-submitted recipes, photographs, reviews, and moderation rights; handle takedown requests.
- [ ] Review age/eligibility requirements, spam/abuse response and acceptable use language.
- [ ] Clarify whether Eatable is an individual project or operating through a business; publish accurate legal identifiers where required.
- [ ] Make sure no fake claims about ordering, premium plans, restaurant services or partnerships appear in the UI.
- [ ] Check EN and IT legal text for consistency and update any effective dates.
- [ ] Replace the visible draft warning **only after** all required details are complete and approved.
