# Editorial review before publishing

These recipes are original working drafts based on recognized dishes, NOT kitchen-tested or certified traditional formulas.

- Verify family/regional versions, ingredient proportions, and precise cooking times before public release.
- Photos must be added manually by the administrator and must be yours or legally licensed.
- Iranian is a country filter; Persian refers to the cuisine. Transnational dishes are not claims of exclusive culinary origin.
- `diet: unknown` is used for cheese/rennet uncertainty; review labels before publishing.
- `prep_minutes` is an estimate of total active + cooking time; overnight soaking is excluded where explicitly noted.
- Raw meat and poultry must be fully cooked; use appropriate safe internal temperatures. Rice must be safely cooled/refrigerated if held.
- Refrigerated pickles are **not** shelf-stable canned preserves.
- The importer does not check allergen labels. Nuts, sesame, dairy, eggs, wheat/gluten and other allergens should be evaluated.
- Existing database content may already have similar recipes even when prior-pack slug checks passed. No live Supabase read or write was performed.
