# Eatable v28 — Editorial review before publication

1. **Attribution** — Confirm that the specific preparation matches the labeled national cuisine. Shared or diaspora dishes have multiple legitimate versions; the country label is not a statement of exclusive ownership. Some drafts explicitly say “style” or “inspired.”
2. **Ingredients** — Inspect all ingredient IDs, quantities and units. Uploaded photos must match the actual recipe. Ensure new ingredient labels in English and Italian are understandable to home cooks.
3. **Preparation** — Cook-test recipe timing, yield and steps. Some traditional processes (soaking, desalting, proofing, marinating) may need additional advance preparation beyond the indicative total minutes.
4. **Food safety** — Cook poultry to a verified safe temperature (often 74°C / 165°F); ground meats fully; handle shellfish, eggs and rice safely. Adjust instructions for local food-safety guidance and actual appliances.
5. **Allergens** — Review gluten, sesame, nuts, milk, egg, seafood, peanuts and soy. A vegan or vegetarian label is not a guarantee of allergen-free food. Cheeses using animal rennet may not be vegetarian; prefer `unknown` classification until the specific product is checked.
6. **Cultural specificity** — Examples such as an oven chicken “inspired by shuwa” should be described as adaptations, not exact traditional preparations. Regional variants of ful, shawarma, couscous and shakshuka should be labeled specifically and carefully.
7. **Photographs** — Add your own photos or correctly licensed images manually in the admin editor. Do not publish generic images that misrepresent the dish.
8. **Language** — Check every Italian translation and culinary term, especially unusual Arab ingredient names, pastry types and regional techniques.
9. **Duplicates** — The pack was checked against the original demonstration recipes, v9 starter, v18 recipes and completed v27 pack. It was not checked against every live Supabase recipe possibly added since then.
10. **Publish only reviewed content** — The importer is intentionally draft-only.
