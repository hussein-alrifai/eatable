## Suggested workflow

1. Audit existing published entries (including the 18 demo recipes). Replace overly short demonstration instructions with tested, detailed versions.
2. Start with the Lebanese Table: prioritize 15 popular dishes with overlapping pantry ingredients; photograph them consistently.
3. Add 20 common-ingredient international dishes next so What Can I Cook? can suggest genuinely different meals from the same pantry.
4. Expand gradually toward 60–100 verified entries. Use the v8 private editorial checklist per recipe; do not mark everything as reviewed in bulk.
5. Review seasonal, allergen and cultural claims with qualified sources as needed. No automated checklist can replace that judgment.
