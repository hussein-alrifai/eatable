## Before you publish any candidate

- Cook the recipe and record any changes to timings and quantities.
- Verify exact ingredient names, serving yield and allergens independently.
- Check culinary names and any geographic/cultural claims against trustworthy sources.
- Ensure both Italian and English cooking steps say the same thing.
- Use original photographs or properly licensed photographs with documented rights.
- Update diet classifications where appropriate (e.g., dairy, eggs, meat).
- Mark each applicable review field only after it has actually been checked.
- Open the public recipe on desktop and mobile and test its ingredient search matches.

## Recommended writing workflow

1. Import candidates privately at `/en/admin-import`.
2. Open `/en/admin-curated` to edit each draft.
3. Add photographs in the private editor.
4. Use `/en/admin-quality` to record your review checks.
5. Publish one recipe at a time after validation; check English and Italian views.

The 80-title backlog from v8 remains a planning artifact. Do not treat it as an automatically populated production library.
