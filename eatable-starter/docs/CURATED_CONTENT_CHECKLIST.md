# Eatable — Curated Recipe Quality Checklist

Use this checklist for every recipe before public launch. The first launch target is **60–100 curated bilingual recipes**, including a prominent Lebanese collection.

## Content

- [ ] Dish title is correct and understandable in English and Italian.
- [ ] Country/cuisine attribution is accurate; avoid unjustified exclusive-origin claims for shared traditional dishes.
- [ ] Preparation steps have been tested or reviewed by someone who has cooked the dish.
- [ ] All ingredients use the canonical ingredient vocabulary.
- [ ] Amounts, measurement units, servings and total time are realistic.
- [ ] All required ingredients are represented; no dangerous omissions.
- [ ] Vegetarian/vegan labels have been checked ingredient by ingredient.
- [ ] Where relevant, communicate common allergens in the recipe text (this version is not a validated allergy classifier).
- [ ] English and Italian ingredient/step descriptions agree on cooking method and quantities.

## Photography

- [ ] The photograph really shows the stated finished dish.
- [ ] You own the image or have suitable permission/license; retain proof and attribution details offline if required.
- [ ] Natural light, uncluttered background; food is recognizable even at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Landscape-oriented image, ideally 3:2 or 4:3, high enough resolution for desktop cards.
- [ ] JPG, PNG or WebP, no more than 5 MB. Compress before uploading if necessary.
- [ ] Check the crop in both recipe cards and the detail header on mobile and desktop.

## Publishing test

- [ ] Save as draft and verify the photo is private.
- [ ] Publish and verify the card in Explore and recipe detail in EN/IT.
- [ ] Verify ingredient matching in What Can I Cook?
- [ ] For Lebanese recipes, verify placement in The Lebanese Table.
- [ ] Fix or unpublish any recipe that fails these checks.

## Editorial priorities

1. Finish and photograph the strongest 30–40 Lebanese recipes first.
2. Add international recipes sharing common pantry ingredients to strengthen matching suggestions.
3. Build the collection gradually to the planned 60–100 verified bilingual recipes.
4. Only then invest substantially in AI recommendations, subscriptions, and restaurant integration.
